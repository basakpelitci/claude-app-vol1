# User Journey

The journey is designed around the **day 30–40 motivation cliff**. Every stage
has an emotional job, not just a functional one.

---

## Stage 0 — First Launch (minute 0–5)

**Emotional job:** feel understood, not measured.

1. Warm welcome — no forms yet. "This isn't a diet app."
2. **Why I Started** — captured *first*, before any number. Free text +
   optional photo. Stored as the root motivation artifact.
3. Profile: age, height, sex, current weight (BIA panel if they have one).
4. Goal: target weight or body fat %, with the Goal Engine immediately showing
   a *sustainable* projected date (Prediction Engine) — never a crash promise.
5. Activity level, cycle tracking opt-in (private, skippable).
6. First Dream Wardrobe pin suggested: "What are you working toward?"

**Exit state:** personalized targets on screen, one motivation artifact saved,
projected goal date visible.

## Stage 1 — Honeymoon (days 1–14)

**Emotional job:** effortless wins.

- Morning: dashboard shows fresh targets; cycle phase chip if enabled.
- Logging a meal animates Momentum upward instantly — partial credit visible.
- Water and steps count as real wins (Maya's achievable victories).
- Day 3: first AI insight appears ("Your protein is most consistent at lunch").
- Day 7: first weekly Health Score with reasons; first milestone celebration.

## Stage 2 — The Grind (days 15–40) — **the critical window**

**Emotional job:** stay connected when novelty fades.

- **Imperfect day flow:** user overeats at dinner → Momentum shows 74, not a
  broken streak. Copy: "Calories ran high today — protein and water were
  excellent. Tomorrow starts fresh at your normal target."
- **Plateau flow:** scale weight flat for 10 days → Health Score explains:
  "Body fat trending down, muscle preserved, water up. The scale is hiding
  your progress." BIA composition chart is one tap away.
- **Luteal week flow:** cycle phase shifts → Goal Engine raises calories
  slightly, recommends recovery workouts. Copy: "Your body needs more this
  week. Your targets adapted — cravings here are physiology, not weakness."
- Day 30: **Transformation Timeline** unlocks — Why I Started replayed next to
  current trends. Dream Outfit card shows fit progress.

## Stage 3 — Identity Consolidation (days 40–90)

**Emotional job:** "this is who I am now."

- Identity Builder prompts: "You've trained 3× a week for 6 weeks. That makes
  you someone who trains." Achievements reference behavior, not weight.
- Letter to Myself scheduled at day 45, delivered at day 75.
- Predictions tighten; goal date confidence rises; Dream Wardrobe items move
  toward "fits".

## Stage 4 — Goal & Beyond

- Goal reached → celebration sequence, transformation timeline replay,
  Dream Outfit "it fits" moment.
- Goal Engine transitions to maintenance targets automatically.
- New goal or wardrobe collection can begin a new journey without data loss.

---

## Journey-critical moments (design invariants)

| Moment | Rule |
|---|---|
| Any missed/imperfect day | Show what *went well* first; never red, never zero |
| Weight up on scale | Always pair with composition context if BIA exists |
| Cycle phase change | Targets adapt silently; user is informed, not warned |
| Milestone hit | Full-screen celebration, references identity not weight |
| 3+ inactive days | One kind re-entry nudge referencing their "Why", then silence |
